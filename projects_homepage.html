Projects

Projects are the pride and soul of Gamespawn! They are games made with blood, sweat, and tears (hopefully not) made by our brilliant members of the club. Each project is led by usually one (sometimes multiple) lead to which everyone on that team works towards creating a playable game by the end of their 10 week journey. Keep in mind that we are still students with responsibilities outside of Gamespawn so even if it doesn't look the best, all we strive for is something finished, not a masterpiece.
